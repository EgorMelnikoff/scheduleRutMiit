# Privacy Policy
_Last updated: July 28, 2026_

Schedule RUT(MIIT) ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Application, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

By using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect
The Application does not require user registration and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or password.

However, certain information is collected automatically to help improve the Application.

### Usage Information
When you use the Application, anonymous usage information may be collected, including:
- application launches;
- screens viewed;
- feature usage;
- session duration;
- application version;
- device model;
- operating system version;
- language and region settings.

### Crash Reports
If the Application crashes or encounters an unexpected error, diagnostic information may be collected, including:
- crash logs;
- device model;
- Android version;
- application version;
- technical information necessary to diagnose and fix issues.
This information is not used to personally identify you.

## Analytics and Third-Party Services
The Application uses the following Google Firebase services:

• Google Analytics for Firebase
Google Analytics for Firebase helps us understand how the Application is used, which features are most useful, and how the overall user experience can be improved.

• Firebase Crashlytics
Firebase Crashlytics automatically collects crash reports and diagnostic information to help identify and fix technical problems.
These services may collect device identifiers and other technical information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Privacy Policy:
_https://policies.google.com/privacy_

Firebase may process data on servers located in different countries.
